The idea

One voice-first platform for every local problem

Instead of building one small app for one small problem, we built one shared pipeline that any community issue can flow through — so each new domain only needs a category and a responder.

Speak, snap or typeUrdu, Roman Urdu, Punjabi or English
AI understands & verifiesCategory, urgency, duplicates, confidence
The right people actRescue 1122, fire, police, water, NGOs
Citizens see it resolvedLive status, notifications, accountability

How it works

Speak → understand → verify → route → resolve

1
SpeakVoice note, photo or text — or just talk to the AI
2
AI asksUp to 3 short spoken follow-ups with tap-able icon answers
3
AI verifiesLinks duplicates across languages, checks photos, scores confidence
4
RoutesNearest matching responders get a push alert instantly
5
ResolvedClaim → in progress → resolved, citizen notified
Example: “سٹریٹ 5 کے سکول کے باہر گٹر کھلا ہے” → a High-urgency Hazard pin appears live and Rescue 1122 is alerted. A second person types “open manhole near the school on Street 5” → it joins the same pin → Verified → claimed → Resolved.

Inclusion by design

Built for people who cannot read — and everyone else

Women's safety

A silent SOS that listens for danger

Easy to triggerHold the SOS button, press volume 3×, shake the phone, or the app-icon shortcut
Completely silentNo sounds — vibration only — so an attacker isn’t alerted
AI threat detectionRecognises threats like “maar dunga”, “bachao”, “I’ll kill you” in Urdu, Punjabi and English
Police alerted privatelyNearest police get live location and audio evidence — never shown on the public map
Logged for the userEvery SOS is recorded in the user’s own SOS log
